Aurora Tech Park — Incubation programme, winter intake
Deadline: 18 October 2026, 23:59

Who can apply
- Companies founded less than three years before the deadline
- A working prototype that can be demonstrated to the committee
- At least one founder working full time on the product

How projects are assessed
1. Technological novelty
2. Commercial potential
3. Team capability

Timeline
- 18 October: applications close
- 28 October: shortlist announced
- 4 November: pitches to the evaluation committee
- 16 November: results
